# LaunchGuild: Design Partner Tycoon

**Turn your first design partners into customers without building five different products.**

An early B2B founder has an MVP, a handful of design partners and more requests than engineering capacity. LaunchGuild links partner evidence, scoped work, explicit promises and purchase blockers to one reviewable founder decision. An original pixel town makes those relationships visible; a draft scenario never upgrades a customer or creates revenue.

## What the local prototype demonstrates

Three explicitly fictional partners share one persisted record graph. Compare a 20-point Salesforce integration serving two partners with a 46-point custom approval workflow for the largest opportunity. The engine counts each company once: Salesforce plus CSV still covers $38,000 in conditional annual opportunity. It shows reserves, dependency closure, effort ranges and commercial unknowns, then saves a reviewed decision and queues work without fabricating delivery.

Evidence opens beside recommendations. A classification correction checks the current field, preserves unrelated edits and can be undone as a new version. Readiness is met applicable checks divided by applicable checks, with unknowns and critical blockers visible. Nothing is called a conversion probability.

## Built with

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Zod, original SVG pixel art, Supabase SSR/Postgres migrations and RLS, Inngest durable-job adapter, OpenAI Responses adapter, Google Gemma evidence-review adapter, ElevenLabs React voice adapter. The demo uses isolated transactional IndexedDB. All monetary and capacity math is deterministic code.
