6. Restart the server after environment changes. Check configuration flags, then separately perform authenticated read-only provider checks. A configuration flag records variable presence, not credential validity or successful delivery.

## One controlled live test

Use one real reviewed pilot and the exact destinations chosen by the user. Record redacted provider IDs and outcomes in a private proof record; keep personal addresses, numbers, calendar IDs and secret credentials out of public source control.

1. Check one future free appointment window within owner-approved hours and within 180 days. Its duration must exactly match the confirmed operating brief, which accepts 15–120 minutes. Confirm its date, time zone, contact details and SMS consent. Submit one stable UUID booking key. Reopen the Google event and refresh the saved receipt. Retry the same key and verify there is one event and at most one SMS; do not retry an uncertain SMS using a fresh key.
2. Refresh the Twilio message receipt until an actual provider outcome is available. `accepted`, `queued`, `sending` and `sent` do not establish delivery. Record `delivered`, failure or unresolved status as reported, then verify receipt with the consenting recipient.
3. Review the exact email subject/body and enable the single pilot's rule. Trigger one due customer-owned task. Inspect the Resend ID and the signed webhook delivery receipt, or the read-authorized API receipt if no webhook is configured, then verify receipt with the chosen recipient. Confirm the saved provider ID, sender and recipient match. Retry the job and a duplicate signed event; check that neither creates another send or regresses the receipt. Complete the task and check suppression; pause the rule and check future unsent work is cancelled or suppressed.
4. Exercise occupied slots, foreign customer scope, revoked access, changed plan and provider rejection/timeout. Preserve uncertain receipts for reconciliation. These checks must not send to uninvolved customers or alter unrelated calendars.

Provider acceptance is separate from delivery. Delivery is separate from customer satisfaction, business accuracy, revenue or payment. Close the gate only when actual receipts establish the requested outcome.
